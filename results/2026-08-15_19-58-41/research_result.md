Summary — latest EU AI regulation developments in 2025

1) Implementing act adopted and corrigenda
- The Commission adopted and the OJ published one AI‑Act implementing regulation in 2025: Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/454 (adopted 7 March 2025) establishing rules for the scientific panel of independent experts. (SRC6, SRC5)
- Corrigenda to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the AI Act) were published in the OJ in 2025 for several non‑English language versions (examples: OJ entries 9 Oct 2025 and 19 Dec 2025). (SRC18)

2) No delegated acts published in the OJ in 2025
- No Commission delegated acts amending or supplementing Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 were published in the Official Journal in 2025; the scientific‑panel implementing regulation was the only AI‑Act‑specific act published in the OJ that year. (SRC6, SRC17)

3) Key application/compliance dates that took effect in 2025
- 2 February 2025: the AI Act’s prohibitions (Article 5) and certain general provisions (definitions, AI literacy) entered into application. (SRC1, SRC2, SRC17)
- 2 August 2025: obligations for providers of general‑purpose AI models entered into application and Member States were required to have designated national competent authorities/single points of contact and to notify the Commission (and adopt national penalties). (SRC1, SRC3, SRC14, SRC17)
- The bulk of the Regulation was still scheduled to apply from 2 August 2026 (with other transitional dates thereafter). (SRC17)

4) Non‑binding guidance, templates and operational support published in 2025
- Commission non‑binding materials to help implementation included: guidance on the AI system definition (early Feb 2025); Guidelines on prohibited AI practices (C(2025)5052, 29 July 2025); guidance for providers of general‑purpose AI models (18 July 2025); the General‑Purpose AI Code of Practice (10 July 2025); a GPAI training‑data summary template (31 July 2025); and a reporting template for serious incidents involving GPAI models with systemic risk (4 November 2025). These are practical, non‑binding instruments rather than new binding obligations. (SRC8, SRC2, SRC3, SRC11, SRC7, SRC4)

5) Article 73 (serious‑incident reporting) — guidance still pending at end‑2025
- Article 73 required the Commission to issue guidance by 2 August 2025. By the end of 2025 the Commission had published draft materials, carried out consultations and released an incident‑reporting template (4 Nov 2025), but no final binding Commission guidance had been published. (SRC12, SRC4, SRC16)

6) Consultations and draft implementing act work in 2025
- The Commission ran stakeholder consultations relevant to secondary rules: a targeted consultation on high‑risk classification and Annex III content (launched 6 June 2025, responses by 18 July 2025) and a public consultation (opened December 2025) on a draft implementing act to set common rules for AI regulatory sandboxes. These were preparatory steps for future delegated/implementing acts. (SRC15, SRC13)

7) Operational/administrative implementation steps
- The Commission’s AI Act Service Desk / Single Information Platform (SIP) and related timelines were active in 2025; the Commission launched an AI Act whistleblower tool in late November 2025 to receive confidential reports to the EU AI Office. (SRC1, SRC10)
- Member States were required to designate and notify market‑surveillance and notifying authorities by 2 August 2025; the Commission’s late‑2025 pages showed the list was only partly populated and some notifications were still pending. (SRC14)

8) Digital Omnibus (’AI Omnibus’) proposal
- On 19 November 2025 the Commission tabled a Digital Omnibus / Digital Package proposal (COM(2025)836) containing targeted simplifications and amendments related to AI Act implementation. By year‑end 2025 it remained a Commission proposal under the ordinary legislative procedure. (SRC9, SRC20)

Notes and unresolved items
- Commission materials and the Service Desk timeline reference a 2 May 2025 milestone for codes of practice to be “ready,” but the precise legal basis and implications of that particular date were not resolved in the 2025 material reviewed here. (SRC1, SRC17)
- The research shows several draft or non‑binding instruments and consultations in 2025, but final binding delegated or implementing acts (other than Implementing Regulation 2025/454) were not published in the OJ in 2025. (SRC6, SRC17)

Bottom line: 2025 was focused on putting in place the AI Act’s governance architecture and practical implementation tools — the Commission adopted the scientific‑panel implementing regulation, issued multiple non‑binding guidance documents and templates, opened consultations (notably on sandboxes and high‑risk classification), saw key obligations start applying on 2 Feb and 2 Aug 2025, launched operational support (whistleblower tool, Service Desk), and tabled a Digital Omnibus proposal to simplify implementation (all supported by the sources below).
