Core build (high‑MMR / pro‑weighted signal in 7.41e):
- Core progression observed in 7.41e (7000+ MMR / pro‑weighted sample): Magic Wand → Power Treads → Yasha (into Sange & Yasha) → Black King Bar (BKB). Dota2ProTracker labels these as “core” (>50% for the core build) and reports median buy times roughly: Magic Wand ~4m, Power Treads ~7m, Yasha ~16m, BKB ~25m. This is the safest baseline build in the current patch. (SRC8)

Situational / low‑frequency but sometimes useful items (7.41e signals):
- Hurricane Pike / Dragon Lance: now give reduced ranged bonus and Pike push/cast range is smaller — Pike is low frequency on Razor in 7.41e (~2.2% pick in the D2PT sample) and is a late, situational pickup. (SRC2, SRC1, SRC8)
- Lotus Orb, Scythe of Vyse, Monkey King Bar, Mask of Madness: appear in the high‑MMR/pro sample at low pick rates and late median buy times — useful situationally but not core in this sample. (SRC8)
- Sange & Yasha vs Manta: the D2PT core path shows Yasha into Sange & Yasha in the sampled builds; community guides also list Manta and other Yasha upgrades as common situational options. Exact win‑rate comparisons by path were not extracted here. (SRC8, SRC11)
- Maelstrom/Mjollnir, Shiva’s, Butterfly, Satanic, Abyssal, Blade Mail, Nullifier: these items are commonly mentioned in community/pro guides and are viable in various matchups; 7.41/7.41e changed several of their stats/recipes (see notes below) which affect timing/efficiency. (SRC11, SRC1, SRC5)

Patch item changes that affect Razor item choices in 7.41 → 7.41e:
- Dragon Lance / Hurricane Pike: ranged attack bonus reduced (+140 → +130) and Pike push/cast range reduced — slightly weakens the value of Pike/Lance for range/tether spacing. (SRC2, SRC1)
- Blade Mail: recipe/components changed (affects early path and armor component). (SRC1, SRC2)
- Butterfly: agility/damage numbers adjusted. (SRC5)
- Abyssal Blade: strength bonus adjusted. (SRC5)
- Satanic: Unholy Rage cooldown increased in 7.41e (30s → 40s) — makes Satanic a less flexible lifesteal choice for Razor (longer downtime). (SRC5)
- Shiva’s Guard: recipe cost changed (total unchanged). (SRC5)
- Aghanim’s Shard / Storm Surge interactions: there were fixes/adjustments to how Razor’s Storm Surge/Aghanim’s Shard interacts (7.41d/7.41e). (SRC7, SRC4)

Role context:
- Razor is most frequently played Offlane in the 7.41e samples (Offlane ~53.3% of games), with Mid and Carry being secondary roles. Item priorities can shift by role; the D2PT core above reflects high‑MMR/pro‑weighted matches where Offlane/Mid roles are common. (SRC9, SRC8)

Practical guidance for item selection in 7.41e:
- If you want a safe, meta‑aligned build: follow Magic Wand → Power Treads → Yasha → Sange & Yasha → BKB. Use the median buy times above as timing goals (Wand ~4m, Treads ~7m, Yasha ~16m, BKB ~25m). (SRC8)
- Buy BKB vs heavy control/disable lineups — it is part of the documented core. (SRC8)
- Consider defensive/utility items (Lotus, Hurricane Pike) situationally, but note they are low frequency on Razor in the high‑MMR sample; their value depends on enemy draft (e.g., heavy single‑target lockdown, save needs, or kiting threats). (SRC8, SRC2)
- Re‑evaluate Satanic as a pickup: the 7.41e cooldown increase makes it less attractive as a frequent lifesteal insurance item — weigh its later‑game value against longer downtime. (SRC5)
- Neutral items: common neutral picks such as Possessed Mask or Chipped Vest appear in 7.41e high‑MMR data for Razor; neutral choices can be impactful but full ranked neutral‑item win rates by tier were not exported here. (SRC8, SRC10)
